## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an elevated, omnichannel electronics shopping experience that merges the precision minimalism of the Apple Store with the expansive retail clarity of premier electronics destinations. Built to elevate customer trust and drive conversion, the aesthetic departs from cluttered, banner-heavy marketplaces in favor of an expansive, gallery-grade presentation.

### Emotional Persona & Narrative
- **Pristine & Technocentric:** Ample negative space allows flagship devices, industrial design details, and photography to stand front and center.
- **Authoritative & Trustworthy:** Deep royal navy foundations project enterprise reliability, financial security, and warranty assurance.
- **Energized Action:** Targeted hits of vibrant crimson red guide purchase intent, highlighting promotional tags, stock alerts, and high-priority conversion flows without overwhelming the visual field.

### Design Movement: Modern Minimalist Commerce
The visual philosophy pairs razor-sharp typographic hierarchy with subtle surface division. Rather than heavy dividing rules or aggressive fills, content chunks are organized inside low-contrast slate-bordered cards with generous corner radii and multi-tiered ambient lighting.

## Colors

The palette employs a stark, deliberate contrast model designed to maximize clarity in high-density product specifications.

### Key Roles & Distribution
- **Canvas & Backing (`#FFFFFF` & `#F8FAFC`):** The primary view relies on pristine white backgrounds to give hardware packaging and product rendering true color fidelity. Secondary bands and filter drawers use an off-white tint (`#F8FAFC`) to segregate administrative and secondary utilities.
- **Primary Navy (`#0B1C48` & `#1A2E6C`):** Used for top-level navigation headers, key action triggers, primary product titles, and authority badges ("Certified Partner", "Verified Spec"). It replaces traditional black with a rich, corporate blue tone.
- **Crimson Red Accent (`#E31E24` & `#D91B24`):** Strictly deployed for purchase-driving elements: "Add to Cart", discount percentages, deal countdowns, live inventory trackers, and dynamic price drops.
- **Typography Neutrals (`#0F172A` & `#475569`):** High-contrast Slate-900 ensures readability across dense specification tables, technical comparison sheets, and critical pricing matrices.

## Typography

The type system prioritizes geometric clarity, compact numerical tabular layouts, and high-density readability through the deployment of `Inter`.

### Hierarchical Rules
- **Display & Flagship Headlines:** Styled with negative tracking (`-0.025em` to `-0.015em`) and semi-to-bold weights to echo precision engineering and premium hardware craftsmanship.
- **E-Commerce Specific Roles:** Dedicated tokens (`price-hero`, `price-card`) specify heavy numeric weights and tighter line-heights to eliminate whitespace drift around currency symbols.
- **Specification Microcopy:** Technical tables and chip badges utilize `label-md` and `label-sm` with slight positive tracking to preserve legibility when rendered in all-caps or bordered containers.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is anchored by an adaptive 12-column grid designed for modular commerce widgets, feature comparison spreads, and dense hardware listings.

### Responsive Breakpoints & Adaptations
- **Desktop (1280px+):** 12 columns, 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters, dynamic auto-centering container capped at `1440px`. Product cards sit comfortably across 4-column or 3-column spans.
- **Tablet (768px – 1024px):** 8 columns, 16px (`1rem`) gutters, 24px edge margins. Multi-column specs break down into horizontal swipe carousels or 2-column card layouts.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, 12px (`0.75rem`) gutters, 16px (`1rem`) edge padding. Dense 2-up product grids shift touch targets to a minimum vertical footprint of 44px.

### Spatial Rhythm
Inner-card gaps maintain a strict 12px to 16px cushion between product media, brand typography, rating reviews, and call-to-actions, preventing visual collisions within dense shopping screens.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses a refined hybrid approach: low-contrast borders (`border-slate-200`) establish structural boundaries at base state, while progressive ambient diffusion conveys interactive depth without heavy visual weight.

### Surface Tiers & Shadow Physics
- **Base Level (Resting Cards):** Elevated solely via crisp boundary definition (`1px solid #E2E8F0`) over `#FFFFFF` or `#F8FAFC`. Zero or minimal shadow (`0 1px 2px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`) ensures an uncluttered, high-end showroom look.
- **Interactive State (Hover/Focus):** Lifts with `shadow-md` to `shadow-lg` (`0 12px 24px -4px rgba(11, 28, 72, 0.08), 0 4px 6px -2px rgba(11, 28, 72, 0.03)`), utilizing a very subtle Royal Navy ambient tint rather than harsh neutral blacks.
- **Overlays & Floating Modals (Cart Drawer, Filter Sheet, Buy Bar):** Deploys `shadow-2xl` (`0 25px 50px -12px rgba(11, 28, 72, 0.18)`) combined with a frosted backdrop filter (`backdrop-blur-md bg-white/90`) to maintain orientation within long catalog lists.

## Shapes

The design system standardizes on generous, contemporary curvature to offset the technical rigidity of electronics specs.

### Geometric Application
- **Product & Content Cards:** Mandate an elegant `rounded-2xl` (16px / `1rem`), providing a smooth perimeter that softens device silhouettes.
- **Interactive Buttons & Badges:** Primary and secondary conversion buttons implement `rounded-xl` (12px / `0.75rem`) for responsive tactile affordance. Offer pill tags, filter chips, and stock chips use fully rounded pill profiles (`rounded-full` / 9999px) for fast scanning.
- **Inputs & Form Controls:** Match interactive buttons at `rounded-xl` (12px) to guarantee optical uniformity across sticky checkout interfaces and newsletter modules.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (Add to Cart / Buy Now):** Background `#E31E24`, foreground `#FFFFFF`, font weight `600`, radius `rounded-xl`. Hover transitions to `#D91B24` with subtle vertical translation (`-1px`) and `shadow-md`.
- **Secondary Action (View Details / Compare):** Background `#0B1C48`, foreground `#FFFFFF`, radius `rounded-xl`. Hover shifts to `#1A2E6C`.
- **Tertiary / Ghost:** Border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, background `#FFFFFF`, text `#0F172A`. Hover transitions background to `#F8FAFC` and border to `#CBD5E1`.

### Product Cards
- **Structure:** Solid `#FFFFFF` background encased in `1px solid #E2E8F0` with `rounded-2xl` corners.
- **Image Staging:** Nested in a light gray-tinted media container (`#F8FAFC`) with balanced padding (`space-md`) to ensure transparent device assets float consistently.
- **Discount & Tagging:** Positioned absolute top-left; crimson discount tags (`bg-[#E31E24] text-white text-[11px] font-bold px-2 py-0.5 rounded-full`) paired with navy status chips (`"NEW" / "5G READY"`).
- **Price Block:** Primary price highlighted in `price-card` (`#0F172A`), strike-through original MSRP in `#94A3B8`, and savings delta highlighted in `#E31E24`.

### Chips & Filter Pills
- **Unselected:** Background `#F8FAFC`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, text `#475569`, `rounded-full`, padding `0.375rem 0.875rem`.
- **Selected / Active:** Background `#0B1C48`, border `1px solid #0B1C48`, text `#FFFFFF`, font weight `600`.

### Form Fields & Inputs
- **Base State:** Background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, corner radius `rounded-xl`, padding `0.75rem 1rem`, placeholder `#94A3B8`.
- **Focus State:** Border color `#0B1C48`, outer focus ring with soft glow `0 0 0 3px rgba(11, 28, 72, 0.1)`.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- **Style:** 20px x 20px with `rounded-md` (checkbox) or `rounded-full` (radio).
- **Active Fill:** `#0B1C48` primary fill with crisp white icon checkmarks; outer boundary in `#CBD5E1` when unselected.

### Domain-Specific Components: Specification Tables & Bank Partner Strips
- **Hardware Spec Matrix:** Alternating subtle row striping (`#FFFFFF` and `#F8FAFC`), crisp horizontal dividing lines (`#E2E8F0`), label column in `#475569`, value column in bold `#0F172A`.
- **Finance & EMI Partner Badges:** Bordered minimal cards (`border-slate-200 bg-white p-3 rounded-xl`) displaying structured lending logos (Bajaj, IDFC, HDFC) with small microcopy (`label-sm`) declaring "Zero Cost EMI Available".
